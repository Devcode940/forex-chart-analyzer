## 2026-03-30 - Estimator Tuning and Parallel Cross-Validation in MLEnsemble
**Learning:** `GradientBoostingClassifier` without feature subsampling (`max_features=None`) on high-dimensional feature spaces (50 features) evaluated across multi-fold cross-validation is a dominant bottleneck, taking over 30 seconds for 2,200 samples. Using `max_features='sqrt'` along with tuning trees and parallelizing `cross_val_score(..., n_jobs=-1)` while setting underlying estimator `n_jobs=1` prevents thread oversubscription and drops validation latency from ~38s down to ~5.9s without degrading classification accuracy or CV estimates.
**Action:** In scikit-learn ensemble pipelines, always combine feature subsampling (`max_features='sqrt'`) on tree-based gradient boosters with parallel CV fold evaluation (`n_jobs=-1`), ensuring base estimators use `n_jobs=1`.
